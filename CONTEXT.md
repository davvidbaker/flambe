# Flambe

A trace of work over time, shared by a person and coding agents.

## Language

**Unstarted activity**:
An activity that is already on a thread and has not begun.
_Avoid_: Future activity, todo, ghost

**Scheduled activity**:
An unstarted activity with a planned time, drawn on its thread at that time.
_Avoid_: Ghost, future activity

**Limbo**:
Where ideas, tasks, and plans go to die: an unstarted activity with no time, or
an activity that is currently suspended.
