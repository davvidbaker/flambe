description: Audit and repair missed Flambé tracking in this session
---

Follow the explicit reconciliation workflow in the Flambé skill. Audit the
current session and accessible subagents against the selected trace, then
submit one reconciliation proposal. Use `flambe reconcile` to read the trace
version and `flambe reconcile --file <proposal.json>` to apply it. Keep source
logs local and report gaps alongside the resulting repairs.
